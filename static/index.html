Our Story

A Few Months of Passion for Farming

We started our crab farm with a simple goal — to raise the freshest mud crabs and bring them straight to your kitchen, with no middleman, no compromise.

  • 🌊
    Sustainably Farmed

    Our mud crabs are raised in clean, controlled water ponds in Andhra Pradesh, fed on natural diet for premium quality.

  • 🧊
    Packed Fresh

    Every order is cleaned, iced, and carefully packed right after harvest to keep the crabs at their freshest.

  • 🚚
    Direct Delivery

    We deliver directly to your door — no cold-chain breaks, no delays. From our farm to your table.
